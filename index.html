Procedural Planets
Godot · GDScript

Whole planets from a couple of noise functions

Planets are generated from start to finish: terrain from layered noise, surface normals computed for lighting, then biomes assigned by sampling temperature and moisture fields, meaning deserts, tundra and forest areas come from mathematical equations and aren't hard coded.

Terrain Constructed from multiple varying noise functions and masks
Lighting Done from surface normal calculations
Biomes Resulting from temperature and moisture combinations
Procedural Generation Seed based worlds creating vastly different results
