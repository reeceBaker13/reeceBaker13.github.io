Procedural Planets

Godot · GDScript

Whole planets from a couple of noise functions

Planets are generated from start to finish: terrain from layered noise, surface normals computed for lighting, then biomes assigned by sampling temperature and moisture fields. This means deserts, tundra and forest areas come from mathematical equations rather than being hard coded.

Terrain Constructed from multiple varying noise functions and masks.
Lighting Calculated from surface normal information.
Biomes Resulting from temperature and moisture combinations.
Procedural Generation Seed-based worlds create vastly different results.
Procedural generation Shaders & normals Game engine Perlin noise functions
